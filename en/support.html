Frequently asked questions

  • Is the app free? Yes, entirely, and with no ads.
  • Does it work offline? Mostly, yes: adhkar, prayer times, reminders and progress. The contact form needs the Internet.
  • How do I change my city for prayer times? Settings → City.
  • How do I turn on the dark theme? Settings → Theme (by default, the app follows your phone).
  • What happens to my data? Your sessions, progress and city stay on your phone. If you write to us, your message (and your email, if you give it) is sent to us. Anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off in Settings, help us improve the app. Everything is explained in the privacy policy.

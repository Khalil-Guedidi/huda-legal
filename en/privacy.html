Privacy Policy - Huda

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Version française

Huda is an adhkar (remembrance) app designed to work on your phone, with no account and no advertising. Here, in plain words, is the data involved and what we do with it.

Who is responsible for your data

Huda is published by Khalil Guedidi, sole trader (trading name Hudhudis, SIREN 981 464 068, France).
Contact: huda-app@hudhudis.com

What stays on your phone

Your sessions, progress, plan, city, favourites and preferences are stored on your device. Neither the content of your sessions nor your city is sent to a server.

  • Your city is only used to calculate prayer times, directly on your phone. No GPS location is used.
  • Reminders are scheduled by your phone, with no notification server.

When you write to us (contact form)

The contact form is optional. If you use it:

  • Data sent: your message and, if you enter it, your email address. No session, city or progress data is attached.
  • Why: to reply to you, and to fix the errors you report. Without an email address, we cannot reply.
  • Legal basis: your consent, which you give by sending the message.
  • Through whom: the message goes through SimplyForms, a form delivery service established in the European Union (Czech Republic, hosted in Germany), before reaching our inbox (huda-app@hudhudis.com). SimplyForms does not keep the messages: it forwards them by email, then deletes them. No transfer outside the European Union. See its data protection documentation.
  • How long: 12 months in our inbox, after which the messages are deleted.

Anonymous usage statistics

To know what really helps (for example, how many morning sessions are completed) and to improve Huda, the app sends anonymous usage statistics. You can turn them off at any time: Settings › "Help improve Huda". Once they are off, nothing more is sent.

  • Data sent: usage events (for example "morning session completed", "reminder opened", onboarding step reached), with a few details needed to read them, including the practice level chosen at the start and whether you read Arabic; a random identifier specific to the installation, sent only in an irreversibly encrypted (hashed) form; the app version, the platform (iOS or Android) and the main system version, and the app language.
  • Never sent: your name, your email, your city, your location, your time zone, your phone model, the text of your messages or the content of the supplications. No advertising identifier.
  • Why: to understand how Huda is used, in order to improve it. Never for advertising, never to follow you from one app to another.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app. You can object at any time with the switch in Settings.
  • Through whom: TelemetryDeck, an analytics service established in Germany (European Union), designed to receive only anonymised data. See its privacy policy.
  • How long: according to TelemetryDeck's retention period, currently 3 months for our plan.

Internet connection

Most of Huda works without a connection: adhkar, prayer times, reminders, progress. The Internet is only used to send the contact form, the anonymous statistics (if they are on) and to open external services (app store, sharing).

No account, no advertising, no tracking

Huda asks for no sign-up, shows no advertising and includes no advertising tracking tool. Your data is neither sold nor shared for commercial purposes.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your messages, their correction or their deletion, and object to their processing, by writing to huda-app@hudhudis.com. You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting past exchanges.

Uninstalling the app erases all the data stored on your phone.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or with the authority of your country (in the United Kingdom, the ICO).

Children

Practising adhkar requires no personal information. The contact form is optional; we do not seek to collect data about children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page is updated. Any new data collection would only start after this page has been updated.
